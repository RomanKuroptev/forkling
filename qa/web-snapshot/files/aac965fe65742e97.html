�BRING BACK THE OLD SANDBOX is a joke esolang developed with the goal of it being a "memorial language", but has failed at its goals, which was made by User:Tommyaweosme.

Commands

R prints "BRING BACK THE OLD SANDBOX"

Any other character else does nothing
