# Decisions

Dated log of choices that shape the project, newest first. Add an entry when a decision is made, with the reason. Dates are from the commit that introduced the decision.

## 2026-11-19: The source list of the audit harness (audit QS, lane S)
- **`qa/sources.json`, not `qa/coverage.json`.** Spec 9.4 puts the sources in `qa/coverage.json`; steps.md names `qa/sources.json`, and steps.md wins. `coverage.json` stays Phase 2's mockup file.
- **Lemmy memes is `trial failed`, not a `code` fix.** Spec 9.4 calls Lemmy "a passed trial that just needs wiring", but its report (`docs/spec/w2-trials/lemmy.world.md`) fails readable at 63% (mark 80%). The report wins: it is for Roman, recommended "rerun the trial against a text-heavier community, or drop". Mastodon trends: Roman, recommended "drop: robots.txt blocks ClaudeBot by name". Show HN: Roman, recommended "drop" (10% of items have 120 words; the projects are on sites Merge may not read).
- **Show HN counts as a pulse source** (spec 6.1 lists it among the feeds, "the daily pulse"), so it is `major` like Lemmy and Mastodon. Lobsters (maker, blocked by robots.txt) is `minor`.
- **A major that waits for Roman (`fixKind: roman`) does not fail a PR.** No PR can make his choice, and it would hold every PR until he decides. It stays in the report; a blocker always fails.

## 2026-11-19: Triage keeps its state in the issues, pictures on an orphan branch (audit QT, lane T)
- **Clean runs and fix tries live in a hidden line in each issue body** (`<!-- qa-state {...} -->`). The issue is already the one record per fingerprint, so any PC can run triage and no state file is committed. A `qa` issue without that line (the hand-filed `phase-2` issues, the digest) is never touched.
- **"The evidence changed"** means a change in severity, the `what` text or the evidence file names. Picture bytes are left out: a re-render differs by a pixel every run and would comment every night.
- **A missing finding counts as clean only in an area the run looked at** (`qa-out/run.json`), and not where a check did not run, so a nightly run without the explorer cannot close a world issue.
- **A fingerprint that comes back reopens its closed issue** instead of opening a second one. **`info` findings never become issues** (measured, no action; they go in the digest).
- **Pictures:** the issues API takes no uploads, so PNG evidence is committed through the contents API to an orphan branch `qa-evidence` of the forkling repo (public) and linked from the issue. Only `pnpm qa triage` writes there, only `.png` files under safe paths; never `main`, never Merge's repo.
- **Fix PRs from `/qa` merge themselves, gated** as Roman's audit prompt ([`docs/spec/audit/prompt.md`](spec/audit/prompt.md), "Merge rules") says, which is more specific than the general CLAUDE.md line about engine PRs: code after green CI including `qa.yml` (`gh pr checks` exits 0 with checks present); look after that and a separate look reviewer passing the before and after pictures; prompt after the written checks, a 3-day real run and a reviewer agent. `qa-rules` and `needs-roman` PRs are never merged by Claude.

## 2026-11-19: The audit harness is its own package, `apps/qa` (audit Q0)
- **Why.** The harness measures forkling without steering Merge (audit spec 1). Keeping it in its own package keeps sim pure and keeps every score and finding out of the mind's code paths.
- **What.** `@forkling/qa`, run as `pnpm qa <command>` (`qa`, not `audit`, because `pnpm audit` is built in). Every check returns findings in one Zod format (`apps/qa/src/report/finding.ts`); a check that throws or returns a malformed finding gives `qa.check-did-not-run` (major), never a silent pass. A new blocker or major fails `qa.yml`. Pictures are PNG via pngjs (pure JS, works the same on Windows and Linux).

## 2026-11-17: Prices that favour what you can see, 300 points a day, a formula on Sundays (spec v4 step 2, slice B)
- **Why.** Merge held 420 of 450 points and wrote about 4 pages a day, because a page cost 1 point and was always on offer, while a sketch cost 50 against a real cost of about $0.012. Roman decided on 16 November that visible things are priced under their cost and standalone text over it.
- **What.** The table of spec-v4 5.1 (`PRICES`): sketch 30, full work 150, found 80, expand 50 or 20 earned, trip 3, tend 2, revisit 1, caption 1, page 10, deep 30, reply and rest free. Daily points 300, most held 1,500. A write costs a caption's 1 point when its target is a place or a work, else a page's 10. The old one-price `write` and `foundRequest` keys of a stored table are read and ignored.
- **The Sunday step** (from 20:00, after the weekly review): prices again from the real mean cost of each kind over the last 14 days, times its factor (sketch and full work 0.6, expand, trip, tend and revisit 1, found = the found call plus the kit build), at most 25% a week, never under 1, at least 5 calls; and the daily points from `max(100, round10((cap - overhead x days) / days x 1000 x 0.9))`. This replaces the weekly reset of audit PR #173 (the 7-day window, the floor of 20 on a sketch and the 3-call minimum are gone).
- **The ledger keeps only its last 500 calls** (about 3 days), so the formula's 7 full days could never be read from it. The mind state keeps each past day's overhead (`overheadDays`, last 14), read from the ledger the day after. Until 7 days are on record the daily points stay at 300.
- **Daily points, the figure (rule 7).** From Merge's `brain/budget.json` of 17 November: stored `capUsd` 12.6, overhead (thought, decide, morning, reflection, bedtime, memory, screen, goalreview, weekly, look) about $0.085 a day (16 Nov $0.069 over part of a day, 17 Nov $0.101). The formula gives round10((12.6 - 0.085 x 30) / 30 x 900) = **300** at the stored cap of 12.6, and **370** at a cap of 15. Both are inside 200 to 400, and 300 is the starting figure. Merge's `budget.json` is not touched here; the cap raise is made at release time.
- **Replay of Merge's last 7 days under the new prices** (from its step timeline, 11 to 17 November; a write counts as a caption only when its target is a place or a work id, so most count as pages, which is the cautious reading). Points the steps cost, old table against new, per day: 11 Nov 9 against 18; 12 Nov 23 against 59; 13 Nov 122 against 127; 14 Nov 128 against 151; 15 Nov 87 against 130; 16 Nov 80 against 105; 17 Nov 278 against 241. In plain words: on quiet days pages are now the dearer part (7 pages on 14 and 15 November cost 70 points where they cost 7), and on the day Merge made 5 sketches the bill falls from 278 to 241 because a sketch is 30 and not 50. Every day stays well under the 300 a day it now gets (under the old 150 a day, 17 November was 278 and was paid from the 420 saved up), so Merge could also have afforded a found (80) and an earned expand (20) on any of those days.
- **Expected monthly model total.** Overhead about $0.085 x 30 = $2.55. Points are nominal ($0.001 each), and a page, a caption and a trip are over or at their real cost while a sketch is priced at 0.6 of it, so a day that spends its whole 300 points on sketches would cost up to $0.50 in real money. In practice (17 November: $0.24 real for 184 points of things) the expected total is $9 to $12 a month against the $12.6 cap, and under $15 even at the raised cap. The budget guard, the pace rule and the Haiku fallback stay the real brake, not the points.
- **Migration.** Points in hand are kept as they are; the new maximum of 1,500 applies from the next wake. Merge has no stored price table, so the new starting table applies at once.

## 2026-11-17: Merge founds its own places, builds start at once (spec v4 step 1, slice C)
- **Why.** After six live days Merge had never founded a place: the gate (an interest at 40 on three mornings in a row that no place carries, at most 2 open requests) was never met, and a request then waited 7 days for Roman. Roman decided on 16 November that making earns space and Merge founds from the kit.
- **What.** `foundCheck` keeps only the rails: no place of an earlier found still under construction, room in the world (64 places), and the existing 1 found in 7 days. `WAKE_TOP_MIN`, `FOUND_STREAK_DAYS`, `recordWake` and `BUILDING_HOLD_DAYS` are gone (`wakeTop` stays optional in the schema so old files parse). `FALLBACK_DAYS` is 0: the kit build is due as soon as the founding text is written, by the same fallback path. A found costs 80 points (`PRICES.found`, fixed, not reset from the ledger). The found call is not told an interest; it picks 1 to 3 tags from existing topics.
- **Texts.** Merge no longer asks Roman. A place's history says "Founded by Merge, built by Merge from the kit." (the old line still counts as kit-built). `/requests/` no longer lists place requests, only gifts, wishes and the weekly requests; `/places/` shows an open founding as being built.

## 2026-11-17: The sandbox is a root-owned socket unit, not `systemd-run` from the mind (spec v4 step 3)
- **Why.** Step 3 said the mind starts Chromium "through `systemd-run`", allowed by "one sudoers or polkit line". Neither works safely here. The mind service runs with `NoNewPrivileges=true`, which stops sudo (a setuid program) from working at all, and switching that off would weaken the service that holds the keys. A polkit rule can let `forkling` start transient units, but it cannot limit their properties, so the mind could start one as `User=root`: that is root.
- **What.** Two unit files that root installs from the deployed release: `forkling-sandbox.socket` (a Unix socket, `/run/forkling-sandbox.sock`, group `forkling`, mode 0660, one connection at a time) and `forkling-sandbox@.service`, which carries every property step 3 requires (`User=forkling-build`, no network, 1 GB, one CPU, 30 seconds, the scratch folder the only place it may write, HOME and PATH only) plus `InaccessiblePaths` for the engine, the world and `/etc/forkling`. The mind connects, sends the files, and reads back the result. The properties are fixed in a root-owned file, not passed by the mind, and the mind gains no privilege at all.
- **Also decided.** The project's files are served to the browser from the runner's memory (no copy on disk; the scratch folder holds only Chromium's own profile and temporary files, and is emptied after every run, even a killed one). `timedOut` is true when the unit is killed at 30 seconds and also when a page is stuck and never draws a frame. Chromium's own sandbox stays on; if it needs an address family beyond `AF_UNIX` to start, the unit is not widened: the proof shows it and Roman decides.

## 2026-11-17: Prices are reset every Sunday from the ledger (audit 6)
- **Why.** A sketch was priced at 50 points ($0.05) but the day's four Sonnet sketches cost $0.093 in all, about $0.023 each, so the allowance (150 a day) ran dry on money that was never spent. The allowance header always said prices would be reset from the ledger each Sunday; it was not built.
- **What.** On a Sunday, awake, once (`pricesStep`, `pricesDue`, `pricesFromLedger`): each price becomes the average real cost of its calls over the last 7 days, in points of $0.001, rounded up, never below 1, and **a sketch never below 20** (Roman). A sketch and a rework are both sketches; the write step is the `art` kind; a full work is its build (`tinker`) plus its share of the `repair` calls; a trip is up to 6 reads at the average `read` cost; replies and rest stay 0. The daily 150 and the 450 cap are not touched. The old and new prices go to the tick log.
- **The ledger keeps only its last 500 calls** (about 3 days at ~160 thoughts a day), so the window is whatever is left of the 7 days. A kind with fewer than 3 calls there keeps its old price (Roman), instead of being reset from noise.
- **Where the prices are read.** `mind.prices` (absent until the first reset: the starting table stands) feeds the plan check, the points a step spends, the decide prompt's price line and the numbers in the system prompt's verb list. The system prompt text is the same, so the golden files do not change, but its numbers change on a Sunday, which writes the prompt cache once.

## 2026-11-16: Creative pieces on Sonnet, notes answered the same day, a $15 model cap (make Merge fun, slice 4)
- **Models.** Sketches and memes, reworks and replies run on Sonnet 5.5 (they were Haiku). **The write step stays on Haiku**, and notebook pages are 80 words (the model is asked for 80, and a page up to 100 is kept, so a page of 85 is not thrown away): text is the caption now and the main products are visual. Thoughts, decide, reads, looks and tend stay Haiku. The diary was already Sonnet (the sleep rewrite).
- **A fallback, not a stop.** Within $1.50 of the monthly cap the creative calls run on Haiku (`creativeModel`), so the end of a month is plainer sketches for a few days and not a silent Merge.
- **No hard daily sketch cap.** A sketch costs 50 points (a Sonnet call of about $0.05; a point is $0.001) of the 150 a day, so Merge chooses how many: a wallet, not a schedule. A deep sketch is gone (a sketch is always Sonnet). A rework (put a work away, bring it back) is also priced as a sketch for now, because it is marked `size: sketch`: rare (2 a week at most), but dear for what it is.
- **Cut-off answers are named and booked.** `messages.parse` threw when an answer stopped in the middle of its JSON, so the cost never reached the ledger and the tick log said "call failed". The client now reads the text itself: a cut-off is a null with its stop reason and usage.
- **Visitor notes.** A note nobody is near is found after 1 waking hour (it was 6: Roman's note at Home, 10:28, was found at 14:48). A found note that has waited 3 hours makes the next plan include a reply step for it (a plan check; Merge may reply, decline with a reason, or keep it), except in the last hour before bed.
- **No plumbing in its voice.** The prompt's "run as rest" sentence is gone (all nine verbs run). ## 2026-11-16: Reading that actually reads (make Merge fun, slice 3)
All 20 trips began as Merge's own interests typed in as Wikipedia titles. Several were 404s ("Absurd_rigorous_joke_papers", "Multi-directional_shading"), and it then wrote pages as if it had read them. So:
- **No search, no invented addresses.** Wikipedia's robots.txt disallows every search route (`/w/`, `/api/`, `/wiki/Special:`), and Merge never ignores robots.txt. A topic is matched with the titles of pages Merge has really been shown links to (a ring of the last 100 in `mind/state.json`: `seen`), then tried as the Wikipedia page of that name. If neither is a page, the trip says "found nothing to read".
- **The write step is told what it read.** Before, it never saw trails. Now it is shown the pages it really read (in a data block), or told plainly that it read nothing ("Do not write as if you had read about it"), or, for a target no trip was about, that it has not read about it. A place target gets nothing.
- **One trip a day starts from a starting point or a door**, not a topic Merge chose: a plan check (not a switch) asks for it when none has started from one today and 3 or more hours remain. Today's points are kept in `mind/state.json` (`points`) so a replan can still name them. The system prompt lists starting points first and a topic last.
- **A page read in the last 30 days is a way in:** the trip goes on from its links, not ends with nothing.
- **Memes become possible.** A meme had to cite a picture Merge looked at, and Merge never asks to look. An Imgflip format whose record page it read on a trip now counts as the format (role looked); the meme still carries no picture (the work check). Only formats read on a trip count (not merely offered), so the pull-request check can see the same trails later.
- **The planner is shown 12 real page titles** it could go to (seen, not read lately, not already a starting point).
**Why:** the honesty rule ("validators reject... souvenirs that don't match their source") had a hole: a failed trip was recorded as a normal one and the write step could not see it.

## 2026-11-16: Topics change faster, and two variety rules are enforced (make Merge fun, slice 2)
Merge spent five days on one subject (about eight titles, all lidar or elevation), so four things changed:
- **The long goal is looked at every 3 days**, not only on Sunday. One Sonnet call asks the weekly review's own question about the goal (continue, finish, drop or change, and why). It may keep the goal, but it says why, and the reason goes into its memory. (The Sunday review had never run: it first shipped in v0.14.0, after the 15th. The first one is Sunday 22 November.)
- **An obsession ends after 3 days at most**, whatever its weight (`OBSESSION_MAX_DAYS`). Interests fade faster: an ordinary one keeps 85% a day (it was 92%) and an obsession 70% (it was 80%). A topic nobody feeds goes from 60 to under 30 in 5 days instead of 9.
- **Two variety switches** in `mind/config.json`, both off unless Roman turns them on. Without the file, or with one that does not parse, every switch is off and the tick notes why:
  - `sameTopicWrites` (limit 3, days 7). In plain words: if Merge has already written 3 pages about the same subject in the last 7 days, a plan with a 4th write on it is turned down, and Merge is told "you have written about this 3 times in the last 7 days (titles)", and plans again once. "The same subject" is a cluster of pages, not a name: pages that share most of the words they keep coming back to are one subject, so "airborne lidar", "digital elevation model" and "leaf area index" count together. (The repeated-writing measure only sees near-copies: the most alike two of Merge's lidar pages were 0.084 against its 0.35 limit.)
  - `sameWorkTinker` (limit 2). In plain words: Merge may not tinker on the same work a third time in a row. A work counts as the same when its name or idea shares two or more words with the last ones.
  Turning a switch off puts everything back. A plan that is turned down twice falls back to the body's own choices for that moment, as every refused plan does.
**Why:** the loop was invisible from inside. Merge's own pages were too alike in subject, not in wording, so nothing measured saw it. Tuned on the 25 pages of 11 to 16 November (`pnpm --filter @forkling/mind topic-replay --world <clone>`): of the 14 lidar pages of 13 to 15 November, 8 would have been refused, and none of the 3 unrelated ones. The clustering sits on a narrow ground (at 5 shared words the sand pages would join the lidar cluster), so run the replay again as the notebook grows.

## 2026-11-10: World builder, not a pet
Forkling becomes an engine for autonomous AIs that grow a 3D world. Merge keeps a cat's body but has no needs, no temperament and no pet care. It wanders allowlisted sites, keeps souvenirs, tends and grows places, asks Roman to build new ones, and makes sandboxed web works. The spec is `docs/spec/spec.md` (v3.3), and the milestones are W0 to W8. Roman chose the recommended option on its three open decisions: Merge reads allowlisted pages' cleaned text (up to 3,000 tokens), works auto-publish after the check inside a no-network sandbox, and the mind starts fresh with the pet-era journal archived. The cap goes from $10 to $20 a month, including a small server from W3.
**Why:** the pet loop (needs, the bowl, affection) produced an upset cat to whom little happened (`docs/review-2026-11.md`). A world that grows from what Merge reads and makes gives its autonomy something visible to do.

## 2026-10-31: Verbs, not activities
Species packs provide primitive verbs (body and senses, hands, making, growing and food, mind), never activities like "gardening" or "fishing". Behaviour emerges from the mind chaining verbs toward goals it wrote itself.
**Why:** hobbies in code would make Merge a game on rails. The point is believability: what Merge does should only make sense if it has wants of its own.

## 2026-10-31: v1 direction: a pet with a mind of its own
v1 is rewritten around a forkling that lives like a person and does what it chooses with a body, a world and time (goals, memory, beliefs, interests from the real internet, things it makes), judged by eight criteria over 14 days with no human input.
**Why:** the emphasis is on what an autonomous AI can do, not on being a pet game.

## 2026-10-31: Human sleep hours
The forkling keeps roughly human hours in the owner's time zone (Merge: Europe/Stockholm): wakes ~07:00, sleeps ~23:00 to 07:00, with seeded jitter, a weekend lie-in and an optional late night the mind chooses. No LLM calls while asleep except the nightly memory batch.
**Why:** a visitor finds it asleep at night and active by day, and sleep is also the biggest cost saver.

## 2026-10-31: Haiku 5.5 for decisions, Sonnet 5.5 only for reflection
Haiku 5.5 for tick decisions, plans, reading, artifacts, the nightly rewrite, vision and builds. Sonnet 5.5 only for the bedtime reflection and the weekly long-goal review. No other models unless a milestone says so.
**Why:** keeps the monthly spend inside the cap while the two calls that need depth get the better model.

## 2026-10-30: $10 a month cap per forkling
Hard cap enforced by the budget guard in `brain/budget.json`. At the cap the mind stops all LLM calls until the month resets and the forkling carries on with utility AI.
**Why:** anyone should be able to hatch a forkling without worrying about the bill.
